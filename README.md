# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Superstore sales data using **Tableau Public**. The dashboard provides interactive visualizations to understand sales performance, profitability, product categories, shipping modes and geographical sales distribution.

The main objective is to identify business insights and provide data-driven recommendations to improve sales and business performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyse overall sales, quantity and profit.
- Understand geographical sales distribution across states.
- Identify profitable and loss-making product sub-categories.
- Compare sales performance across different shipping modes.
- Build an interactive dashboard using Tableau Public.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- Data Visualization
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains information about:
- Orders and Order Dates
- Customer Details
- Product Categories and Sub-Categories
- Sales and Profit
- Quantity and Discount
- Shipping Modes
- Geographical Information (State, City and Region)

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

| No. | Visualization | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Total Sales | Displays overall sales performance. |
| 02 | Total Quantity | Shows the total quantity of products sold. |
| 03 | Total Profit | Displays overall business profitability. |
| 04 | Sales by State | Geographical representation of sales across states. |
| 05 | Profit by Sub-Category | Compares profit and loss across product sub-categories. |
| 06 | Quantity by Sub-Category | Displays product quantity distribution. |
| 07 | Sales by Ship Mode | Compares sales across different shipping modes and categories. |

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters:

- Region
- Category
- Ship Mode

These filters allow users to explore sales performance based on different business dimensions.

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. **Shipping Performance:** Standard Class contributes the highest sales among the four shipping modes, while Same Day contributes the lowest.

2. **Profitability:** Copiers generate the highest profit, whereas Tables show a significant loss.

3. **Product Performance:** Product sub-categories show different sales and quantity patterns, helping identify high-demand products.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Focus on improving the profitability of loss-making sub-categories by reviewing pricing, discounts and operational costs.

2. Analyse customer preferences and shipping requirements to optimise delivery services and improve sales performance.

## 📝 Conclusion

The Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard provides an interactive overview of business performance through geographical and comparative visualizations.

Tableau helps transform raw sales data into meaningful insights, supporting better business decisions, identifying profitable products and improving overall sales performance.
